Crooked Lines Uploader

Privacy policy · Last updated 10 October 2026

Crooked Lines Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Crooked Lines YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

What it accesses

  • With your permission, it uploads videos, thumbnails and caption files to your YouTube channel and sets their title, description, tags and privacy settings.
  • It does not read your viewing history, subscriptions, comments, analytics, contacts or any other account data.

What it stores

  • A sign-in token issued by Google, kept only on the channel owner's own computer, so uploads don't need a new sign-in every time.
  • Nothing is stored on any server, and no data is collected about viewers or anyone else.

Sharing

No data from your Google account is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video files and their details are sent only to YouTube.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke this tool's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. The only data the tool keeps is the sign-in token on the owner's computer; it is deleted when access is revoked or when the owner deletes the token file, and in any case within 30 days of the tool no longer being used. Videos uploaded to YouTube are managed and deleted in YouTube Studio.
